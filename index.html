About AgriRenta

AgriRenta is a digital platform where equipment owners list agricultural machines for rent, and farmers search, check availability and send a booking request. It was built by BSIT students of ADSSU.

The problem we are solving

Expensive equipment

Tractors, tillers and pumps cost too much for small farmers to buy.

Limited use, hard to find

Some machines are needed only in certain seasons, and farmers rely on word of mouth to find one for rent.

No price transparency

Comparing rental prices, availability and terms is difficult.

Inconvenient process

Farmers contact several owners by call or text to check schedules and prices.

Starting in Bunawan, Agusan del Sur

AgriRenta launches first with local farmers and equipment owners in Bunawan, then expands to other farming communities. Farmers rent only when they need a machine, and owners reach more renters. We will improve the platform based on real user feedback.

One platform for renting farm equipment

Owners list their machines. Farmers search, check availability and send a booking request.

For farmers

  • Search by type, location, price and date
  • See owner ratings and reviews
  • Check that owners are verified

For owners

  • List equipment and set rental prices
  • Manage available dates
  • See each farmer's verification status, rating and reviews

For both

  • One place for the whole rental process
  • Notifications for requests, confirmations and upcoming rentals
